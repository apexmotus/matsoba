Our Story

Every Bloom Tells a Story

Matšoba Floral & Gifting is a proudly Limpopo-based floral studio with three branches — Lebowakgomo, Burgersfort and Ga-Nkwana. We specialise in fresh, high-quality arrangements tailored to your occasion: elegant bridal bouquets, thoughtful gifts, sympathy tributes and everyday flowers that bring a little more beauty into the day.

Every stem is hand-selected and every arrangement made with care — because the moment you're celebrating deserves flowers that feel just as considered.

FAQ

Questions We Often Get

Where are Matšoba Floral & Gifting's flower shops?

We have three branches in Limpopo, South Africa: Lebowakgomo (Lebowakgomo-F, 0737), Burgersfort (R37, 1150) and Ga-Nchabeleng. Each branch card on this page opens its location in Google Maps.

How do I order flowers?

Message us on WhatsApp at 071 329 1054, tap "Order via WhatsApp" on any bouquet in our catalog, or fill in the quote form on this page. The form opens WhatsApp with your details pre-filled.

How much does a bouquet cost?

Our catalog bouquets range from R650 to R950: 20 Mixed Roses & Gyps is R650, 20 Red Roses & Gyps is R850 and the Bridal Bouquet is R950. Custom arrangements are quoted on request.

What occasions do you make flowers for?

Weddings and bridal bouquets, gifts and gift hampers, sympathy and funeral arrangements, table centrepieces and everyday flowers.

Can I order a custom arrangement?

Yes. Tell us your preferred stem count, colours, flowers and occasion, and we'll put together a custom quote on WhatsApp.

Do you deliver?

Include your delivery date and area when you request a quote on WhatsApp, and our team will confirm availability and delivery options for your order.
