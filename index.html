Our Story

Every Bloom Tells a Story

Matšoba Floral is a proudly Limpopo-based floral studio with three branches — Lebowakgomo, Burgersfort and Ga‑Nchabeleng. We specialise in fresh, high-quality arrangements tailored to your occasion: elegant bridal bouquets, thoughtful gifts, sympathy tributes and everyday flowers that bring a little more beauty into the day.

Every stem is hand-selected and every arrangement made with care — because the moment you're celebrating deserves flowers that feel just as considered.
